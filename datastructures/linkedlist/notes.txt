1. get(i) cua ArrayList la O(1) vi mang nhay thang toi o i. get(i) cua LinkedList la O(n) vi phai di tung node tu head qua i lan next.

2. removeLast can ca tail va prev de la O(1): tail cho biet node cuoi o dau, prev cho biet node ngay truoc no de dat next = null. Neu chi co head va next (danh sach lien ket don) thi phai di tu head cho toi node gan cuoi, tuc la O(n) (con ArrayList.removeLast la O(1)).

3. Thi nghiem remove(0) n lan (n = 400,000): MyLinkedList duoi 1 ms, ArrayList 6776 ms. Moi lan n gap doi thi ArrayList mat gap ~4 lan (O(n^2)) vi phai dich ca mang sang trai, con LinkedList chi doi vai con tro (O(1) moi lan). Chon LinkedList khi hay them/xoa o dau hoac cuoi (hoac da cam san node can xoa), chon ArrayList khi hay truy cap theo chi so get(i).
Trong order book: hang lenh o cung mot muc gia, lenh moi xep cuoi hang, lenh khop lay o dau hang, nen LinkedList cho them cuoi va lay dau deu O(1), con ArrayList lay dau la O(n).
